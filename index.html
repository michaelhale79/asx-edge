How recommendations work

BUY requires a strong opportunity score, acceptable risk and confidence, no major negative announcement signal, and no strong bearish short-interest flag. HOLD means evidence is mixed. SELL means the opportunity/risk picture has deteriorated materially. Short interest is a supporting signal only; it never creates a trade by itself.

Tracking

When a BUY/HOLD/SELL signal is first seen, ASX Edge records the price and date on this device. BUY is reviewed after 30 days, HOLD after 20 days and SELL after 14 days. This creates a visible success/failure record instead of letting old calls disappear.
